01 / The story

How we got here

WE DIDN'T PLAN TO BE AN AGENCY.

SCA was founded by Sarthak Thakur and Saksham Thakur after leaving a Bombay shop that promised startups the moon and delivered PowerPoints.


The first client, a Pune coffee brand, needed content shipped every week and wasn't getting it. We shipped it. It worked. They told three friends. Those friends told nine. By month six we had a waitlist, and by month twelve we stopped calling ourselves freelancers.


Today the team is small by design — 14 people, mostly Pune-based, some remote. We keep it small because our unfair advantage is that the people you meet in the pitch are the people doing the work. We intend to keep it that way.

03 / Principles

How we decide

THE NON-NEGOTIABLES.

Arguments we have every week. Rules we don't bend on. The stuff that makes clients who want a churn machine uncomfortable — and the right clients feel right at home.

A

Taste over volume.

We'd rather ship three great things than twelve mediocre ones. Agencies that bill by output die slow deaths.

B

Write it down.

Strategy you can't write is strategy you don't have. Every engagement produces a doc someone on the client side can defend in a meeting.

C

Ship weekly.

Anything that can't be reviewed once a week compounds into a mess by month three. Public roadmap. No black boxes.

D

No vanity metrics.

If it doesn't show up in revenue, pipeline, or brand search, it's not a KPI. It's a screenshot.
