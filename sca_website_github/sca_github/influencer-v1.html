Step 01 — Brief & fit

Brief & fit, before a rupee moves.

Every creator is matched and briefed by a human — by city, niche and intent. Each has worked with us at least twice. We say no more than we say yes.

Step 02 — Contract & track

Contracts and tracking, locked in.

Usage rights, exclusivity and deliverable SLAs on paper. Every post ships with its own UTM link and code, wired into GA4 and Shopify before it goes live.

Step 03 — Live dashboard

One live dashboard, not a month-end PDF.

Reach, clicks, redemptions and engagement update in real time. A single link you can drop straight into a board deck and share with confidence.

Step 04 — Attributed revenue

Attributed revenue, per creator.

We close the loop from impression to order — so you see real cost-per-acquisition by creator, not a blended vanity-reach figure.
