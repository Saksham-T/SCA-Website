02 // PERSPECTIVE

THE DEATH OF THE CONVENTIONAL SURFACING

For years, SaaS and e-commerce companies hid behind clean, rounded-corner templates that felt safe, predictable, and ultimately forgettable. The homogenization of the web has created a visual drought. We are seeing a massive cultural shift back toward editorial layout structures: sharp borders, generous white space, dynamic typographical hierarchy, and structural grids. It is what we call 'brutalist precision'—a design philosophy that values clarity and honesty over cheap visual tricks. When every other brand looks the same, distinctiveness is your greatest competitive advantage.
"Design is not the decoration of a surface. It is the architectural blueprint of how information behaves."
