§ 04 / The math
Retainer vs. building it in-house

You were going to hire for this anyway. Here's what it actually costs.

Option A

In-house team of four

~₹6.4L / mo loaded · 9–12 weeks to hire · 12 months to ramp
  • Strategist, writer, designer, ops — four full-time hires
  • Hiring takes 3 months minimum. First output at month 5.
  • Team churn means restarting once a year
  • You manage the calendar, the reviews, and the reshoots
  • No paid layer unless you hire a fifth person
Option B

Three freelancers + a tool

~₹2L / mo · scattered Slack threads · zero ownership
  • Three people who don't know each other's roadmaps
  • Every brief written from scratch
  • Voice drifts every quarter
  • No one is accountable for outcomes
  • Friday becomes "Where is the deck?"
Option C — recommended

SCA Retainer

One monthly retainer · 4 weeks to first ship · 1 point of contact
  • A pod of four, dedicated. Same faces every month.
  • Brand book signed off in week two
  • Public Notion roadmap. You always know what's shipping.
  • Paid layer included from month one
  • Outcomes tied to your business metrics. We kill what doesn't work.
