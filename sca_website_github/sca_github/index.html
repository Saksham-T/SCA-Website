009 / FAQ
Before you ask

The questions every founder asks us.

What does SeeTusk actually do?+
We're a creative distribution studio. We make your content, run your creator campaigns, and build the websites and tools that turn attention into customers — all shipping on a weekly rhythm.
How do you charge?+
Three ways: a one-off project, a 3–6 month sprint, or an ongoing monthly retainer. Most brands start with a project or sprint and move to a retainer once they see the rhythm working.
How fast do you start shipping?+
First work is usually live within two weeks of kickoff. We'd rather get something good into market and improve it than disappear for a month building a deck.
Do you only work with brands in Pune?+
No. We're based in Pune and shoot across Maharashtra, but we work with brands all over India. Strategy, web and reporting are fully remote.
Can we start with just content, or just creators?+
Yes. You don't have to buy everything. Most brands start with one thing and add the others once it's working.
Who actually does the work?+
The founders and the specialists you meet in the pitch. No account managers relaying messages, no hidden junior team.
