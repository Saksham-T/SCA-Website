01 / The problem

Brands pay creators.
Creators post.
Nothing converts.
Everyone moves on.

The issue isn't the creators. It's the machine around them: mismatched briefs, zero tracking, vanity metrics, and hope-based budgeting.

We fix the machine. We run creator campaigns the way a performance marketer would run paid media.

→

Brief & fit before contract

Every creator is briefed by a human, not a template. We say no more than we say yes.

→

Tracked links, codes and UTMs

If it didn't happen in GA or Shopify, it didn't happen. We measure the path from impressions to orders.

→

Contracts you can enforce

Legal paperwork that actually protects you. Usage rights, exclusivity, deliverable SLAs.

→

One report. Live dashboard.

No end-of-month screenshots. A live link you can share with your board.

02 / Campaign types

Four campaign archetypes.
One system of
accountability.

Launch raids, always-on drips, ambassador tiers and IRL activations — run like paid media. Every archetype comes with full tracking, legal paperwork, and a live dashboard.

Archetype 01

Launch Raid

6–20 creators, 10-day window, one coordinated push. Used for product launches & bursts.

10 days Rs 5–18L
Archetype 02

Always-On Drip

2–4 creators/month, ongoing. Builds brand recall and steady top-of-funnel traffic.

Monthly Rs 3L+
Archetype 03

Ambassador Tier

Long-form deals with 3–5 creators. Usage rights, exclusivity, affiliate-style splits.

6–12 mo Negotiated
Archetype 04

Event & UGC Push

Creator-led activations IRL. Store openings, pop-ups, tastings, launches.

Per event Rs 4L+
